# Contributing to ClaudeInstall

Welcome to ClaudeInstall! We're happy that you would like to add
something to ClaudeInstall. This contribution guide should help you
get started. The guide is not exhaustive: most of it points
to other documents that you will need.

To contribute to ClaudeInstall, find it on the Free-Software friendly
[codeberg forge](https://codeberg.org/ClaudeInstall) and participate over there.
